# 🎬 *You*

![You Netflix Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/fd/You_Season_1.jpg)

*You* is a psychological thriller about Joe Goldberg, a bookstore manager who becomes dangerously obsessed with the people he falls in love with.  
He uses social media and technology to stalk them, believing his actions are out of love — but they often lead to dark and violent consequences.  
The series shows how obsession and manipulation can hide behind the idea of romance.

## 🌟 Main Characters
- Joe Goldberg – Penn Badgley  
- Love Quinn – Victoria Pedretti  
- Guinevere Beck – Elizabeth Lail  
- Marienne Bellamy – Tati Gabrielle  

### ❤️ Why I Like It
I like *You* because it’s full of suspense and mystery.  
Joe’s character is interesting because he’s both smart and creepy at the same time.  
Every episode keeps me curious about what will happen next.
